Wibust

A local cosmetic swapper for Brawlhalla. Nothing leaves your machine.

Download Wibust.exe

Windows 10/11 · 64-bit · about 32 MB · no installer

How to run it

  • Start Brawlhalla and get to a match (or training).
  • Run Wibust.exe. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher — pick "More info" then "Run anyway".
  • Hit Inject once. Wait until the sidebar dot goes green.
  • Open a section, add a rule: what you're using → what you want to see.
  • Rules save automatically and re-apply every match.

Update Patch 11.01

  • New look: full-width top bar, sidebar, one panel per section.
  • Swap rules with a hero picker — pick the skin you play and what it shows as, e.g. Default Mako → General Grievous. Saved per session.
  • Per-rule on/off toggles, drag to reorder, delete.
  • Color and KO/Trail sections are locked off for now (they broke the model).
  • Chakram legends (BladeDancer, CyberVirus, ...) are locked off — the Down Light stance swap fights the swapper.

Notes

  • Reads and writes only in your own process memory. Local only.
  • If something looks off, hit Restore and it puts the originals back.
