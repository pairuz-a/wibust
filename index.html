$ cat patch-11.01.txt

  • New look: top bar, sidebar, one panel per section.
  • Swap rules use a hero picker — pick the skin you play and what it shows as (e.g. Default Mako into General Grievous). Rules run top to bottom; toggle, reorder or delete them.
  • Startup shows each step, and a "Your fighter" row shows what you're wearing and what it will render as. Failures tell you the next step.
  • A skin swap brings that skin's weapon set, a bundle keeps every weapon in it, and custom swing and fire sounds load their soundbank, including from legend select. Restore puts back the weapon you were holding.
  • Deleting a rule, applying a bundle and restoring originals now ask before they act.
  • Colors, KO effects and trail effects are off for now — they broke the model. Chakram legends (BladeDancer, CyberVirus and so on) are off while the Down Light stance swap is worked out.
  • Everything is local and visual only.
