changelog

$ cat patch-11.01.txt

  • New look: top bar, sidebar, one panel per section.
  • Swap rules use a hero picker. Pick the skin you play and what it shows as, like Default Mako into General Grievous.
  • Rules run top to bottom (first match wins) and can be toggled off, reordered, or deleted.
  • The picker now works with the keyboard and tells you what to do when your collection isn't loaded yet.
  • Restore now puts the weapon you were actually holding back.
  • Weapon skins with custom swing and fire sounds now load their soundbank, including when applied from legend select.
  • A skin swap now brings that skin's weapon set, and a bundle keeps every weapon in it.
  • Colors, KO effects and trail effects are off for now. They broke the model.
  • Chakram legends (BladeDancer, CyberVirus and so on) are off. The Down Light stance swap clashes with the swapper.
  • Everything is local and visual only.
